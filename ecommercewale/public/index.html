A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s clear things up.

Still curious? Talk to our team.

What does refurbished mean?

A previously owned device that has been inspected and, where needed, repaired. Actual condition, parts and battery details should be checked on the device listing before purchase.

Will my selling price change at pickup?

The online estimate uses your answers. The final offer follows inspection. Any deduction should be explained for you to accept or decline.

Which cities do you serve?

Our initial coverage is Indore, Ujjain and Dewas. Availability depends on your PIN code and pickup or delivery capacity.

Can I buy a phone right now?

This first version is a preview. You can test purchase enquiries, but online payments and live stock confirmation will be enabled after setup.
